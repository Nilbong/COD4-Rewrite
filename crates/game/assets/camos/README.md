# CoD4 weapon mastery camos

In **Create a Class**, pick any original CoD4 firearm and select the **Camo**
tile beside its preview to open **Camouflage**. When the RPG perk is equipped,
its **Camo** button appears beside the inventory title.
**Gold**, **Platinum** and **Diamond** use the original
game's row artwork and fonts, with preview, swatch and challenge progress.
Primary, sidearm/Overkill and RPG finishes save independently with the class.
The native gold Desert Eagle entry shares its ordinary gun's mastery progress.

| Finish | Requirement for each gun | Completion XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | 150 weapon kills and 150 headshot kills | 1,000 |
| Platinum | Earn Gold and reach 500 total weapon kills | 2,500 |
| Diamond | Earn Platinum on every unique gun in its category | 5,000 per gun |

The RPG uses **150 kills** for Gold and **500 total kills** for Platinum;
headshots are not required. Category progress excludes the duplicate gold
Desert Eagle entry. Kills are credited to the weapon which dealt the finishing
hit on an enemy. Assists, friendly kills and other players' kills do not count.
Old native Marksman/Expert progress is retained when loading a profile.
Challenge XP is awarded once, with an unlock notification.

**CoD4 progression** mode enforces these requirements. **Unlock All** exposes
the finishes for testing, while the Combat Record still shows actual earned
progress. Selecting a locked mastery camo is rejected when loading a class.
These changes apply to the original CoD4 guns, including pistols and the RPG;
Black Ops and World at War guns keep their own finish systems.

Run `test-ak47-platinum.cmd` from the repository root to build the current
game and inspect the original AK-47 wearing Platinum on Killhouse.
Press **I** to inspect both sides. For another map, append `--map mp_crash`.
`test-cod4-camo.cmd m4 diamond mp_crash` tests any original gun and finish.
With no arguments it opens the Diamond AK-47 on Killhouse.
The debug setting is `COD4RW_LOADOUT=ak47@200`; Gold is `@6`, Diamond `@201`.
Debug runs use the existing profile protection and do not change saved progress.

Native Gold models are retained where the original game supplies them.
Other mastery finishes use the native camo-covered body surfaces; pistols and
the RPG use their body material. Miscellaneous parts, optics, reticles and
hands retain their original materials. The existing generated brushed
platinum PNG is embedded and reused across guns, with their original normal
maps, wear details and map reflection probes.

Diamond uses **real raised mesh geometry**, rather than a painted stud image.
Each crystal crown is 3.6 mm wide and 1.2 mm high, with eight flat facets and
an octagonal table. Its inherited skinning follows the gun's animations.
Flat geometric normals and a polished crystal finish give changing glints
as the weapon and camera move. This first Diamond finish uses opaque crystal
facets on gold backing. Geometry is cached and capped at 1,200 studs per coated
surface and 2,400 per model; the facets share the original surface's draw call.
A small procedural menu swatch matches the crowns on gold backing.

`ak47-platinum.prompt.txt` records the final prompt used with the built-in
image generator. The generated source PNG is copied here without resizing.
Diamond geometry and its swatch are generated by game code; no new image
generator asset is required for the raised effect.
